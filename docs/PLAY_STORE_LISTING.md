**Full description (max 4000):**

Stop paying for subscriptions you forgot about.

Subsonar keeps all your recurring payments in one clean list — streaming, music, apps, cloud storage, gym, phone,
internet, insurance — and tells you exactly what they cost you every month and every year. A reminder arrives
before each renewal, so you have time to cancel the ones you no longer use.

WHY SUBSONAR
• See your real monthly and yearly total at a glance
• Sorted by next renewal, with “renews in 2 days” warnings
• Renewal reminders 1–7 days ahead
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly billing
• Any currency — totals are shown per currency
• Spending by category: find where your money goes
• Notes for each subscription (account email, cancel link, plan details)
• Price-hike tracker: every price change is saved, with a “price up 12%” badge, a price history per subscription and the extra yearly cost of all increases
• Find subscriptions automatically in your bank statements (CSV, CAMT.053, MT940) — read on your phone only, never uploaded
• Available in 19 languages, switchable in the app (Settings → Language)
• Export everything to CSV for Excel or Google Sheets
• Beautiful Material You design with light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• Works 100% offline — Subsonar has no internet permission
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Your data stays on your phone (included in your normal Android backup)

FREE
Subsonar is completely free while in early access. Add your first subscription in under 10 seconds.
