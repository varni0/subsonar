# Subsonar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Subsonar (“the app”) is developed by <developer name> (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary
Subsonar does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet access.

## Data stored on your device
The subscriptions you enter (names, prices, currencies, dates, categories, notes) and your app settings are stored
only on your device in the app's private storage. We have no access to this data.

## Backups
If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your
Google account or when transferring to a new phone. This is handled by Google/Android under your Google account
settings and is encrypted by Android; we cannot access it. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

## Exports
When you use “Export to CSV”, the app writes a file to a location you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is
under your control.

## Notifications
Renewal reminders are generated locally on your device. You can disable them in the app or in Android settings.

## Permissions
- Notifications (Android 13+): to show renewal reminders. Optional.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that process data (for example optional cloud backup, in-app purchases via Google
Play, or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the changes will be described here.
